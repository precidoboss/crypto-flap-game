Cultural energy

Yahoo Farmer was born from a contradiction — the boy in the laptop café, fingers moving fast, who still wears his grandfather's agbada to the farm on weekends. Two worlds. One fit.

Our pieces pull from both sides: the flashy hustle-culture of street life and the deep, grounded identity of Nigerian farmland. It's not a costume. It's your actual life.

Caps bent low over screens. Belts that hold everything together. Tops that say nothing and say everything. This is Yahoo Farmer drip — not for the weak, not for the fake.

The DNA

Two worlds,
one wardrobe.

Yahoo boys wear G-Wagons but their mamas still grow cassava. That tension — city flash versus farmland soul — is where Yahoo Farmer lives.

We don't mock either world. We celebrate both. The soil under your nails and the screen glow in your eyes are equally real, equally Nigerian, equally dope.

Every piece is designed in Lagos, inspired by the village, produced for the streets.

🌾
Farm Roots

Soil textures, hoe motifs, harvest gold — our design language pulls from the land your family worked.

📡
Street Signal

WiFi patches, naira symbols, laptop graphics — the hustle aesthetic rendered in fabric and thread.

🇳🇬
Naija First

Designed in Lagos. Every colourway references something real in the Nigerian experience. No appropriation, all authenticity.

🔒
Limited Drops

No mass production. Each collection drops in small batches so the drip stays exclusive and the culture stays sacred.
